The Amazing World of Bryce Canyon

What is Bryce Canyon?

Located in southwestern Utah, Bryce Canyon National Park is famous worldwide for its impressive collection of hoodoos. Formed by a process known as "frost wedging," these irregular, spire-shaped rock formations vary greatly in shape and size and are quite surreal to look at. Also, the views of the night and morning sky are clearer than nearly anywhere else, letting you see a sky full of stars every night.


The most famous hoodoo in Bryce Canyon, known as Thor's Hammer for its unique shape.

Why should I go?

Bryce Canyon has all sorts of amazing things to see, like:

  • Stunning Hoodoos
  • Natural Ampitheaters
  • Hiking Trails
  • Majestic Overlooks
  • Serene Campsites

Not to mention the amazing views of the sky:

  • Sunrise Point is among the most beautiful places to watch the sun rise above the horizon; watching the red rocks catch the first light is truly wonderful.
  • As a certified International Dark Sky park, Bryce Canyon offers views of the night sky that are hard to find anywhere else.

Places to see

If you want to see Bryce's hoodoos up close, consider one of the many trails below the canyon rim!

  • Navajo Loop - A 3-mile hike passing Thor's Hammer and neat rock formations.
  • Wall Street - A dramatic section of the Navajo Loop featuring narrow passages between towering rock walls.
  • Rim Trail - An easy, flat path connecting the viewpoints along the canyon rim.
  • Fairlyland Loop Trail - A quieter 8-mile trail featuring unique formations like the Tower Bridge.

The Tower Bridge, a unique rock formation named for its resemblance to the Tower Bridge in London.
