Have you heard of the hit game Geometry Dash?

Geometry Dash and You

Geometry Dash is a challenging, rhythm-based sidescroller/platformer developed by RobTopGames in 2010. As a tiny icon, you have to traverse long obstacle courses where any mistake sends you right back to the start! Build your resolve or crash out to your heart's content, it's all in the package that is Geometry Dash! You can even build your own levels for others to play - and/or suffer through! Geometry Dash and Me

I have been playing Geometry Dash for over 3 years now and it is by far the best game I've ever played. The challenge it provides its users is punishing, but incredibly rewarding, and I find it incredibly fulfilling to finally complete a level after weeks or months of dedicated playtime. I also love the level editor feature; you can build anything, ZERO exceptions - someone built a game in GD that they were eventually able to sell on Steam - but I enjoy making interesting gameplay and showing it to others.

The Demonlist

The Pointercrate Demonlist is an unofficial site/community ranking the hardest levels and best players in all of Geometry Dash. All the best players can be found on the Pointercrate Stats Viewer, including Zoink, the current best Geometry Dash player. The Demonlist also allows players to submit records and earn points to climb the leaderboard! The Demonlist Discord server is also an excellent and very supportive community that helps players improve at the game quickly.
